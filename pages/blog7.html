author coming out

wafer

hi. yesterday, november 13th, i came out to my mother as gay.

well. it's more like my mom confronted me and i was forced to come out. but the end result is the same.

for some history: my mother has always been critical of queerness. she's religious, russian, old-fashioned, and has battled with acceptance all of her life. in fact, she previously threatened suicide if i came out as gay -- as she wouldn't have any straight children (my brother is also gay and trans and was essentially "kicked" out of the house).

i've always played off trips to see my partner as "visiting a friend" or "going on vacation with multiple friends". but clearly i've dropped some sort of subconcious hints to her.

over the past ~6-8 months, she's asked me about my sexuality multiple times. each time, i avoided the subject or lied to her, telling her i'm not looking for a girlfriend, or that i just went through a breakup with one.

yesterday, i told her my plans of moving out. she asked why i was in a rush to move out, and why i was moving out with this random friend from oregon. a few minutes later, i hear a knock on my door. it's my mom.

she sits down on my bed, reaches for my hand, and asks me if i wanted to tell her anything about my friend, or, more specifically, my boyfriend. it was in this moment my heart dropped. i was already texting my partner about if i should come out, and if so, when.

i took a deep breath and told her i was gay. i cried. really hard. i felt like my world was about to collapse. and in that moment, she hugged me. she told me she loved me and would accept me no matter what. all that she cares for is if i'm happy or not. she told me she's been to queer groups in the past year, made queer friends, and grew her understanding.

i'm still processing all of this, but i feel so relieved that i'm able to take my mom and introduce her to my partner. that i can invite her to my wedding. that i dont have to lie to her. that i am not a failure for who i love. that i am loved.

thanks for reading.
